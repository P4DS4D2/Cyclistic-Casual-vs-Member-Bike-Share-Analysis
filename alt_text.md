# Alt text for charts

**01_seasonality.png**: Line chart of monthly rides in 2019. Member rides stay between about 94k and 403k per month. Casual rides rise from about 3k in February to about 187k in August, when casual riders are 31.6% of all rides.

**02_weekday.png**: Grouped bar chart by weekday. Casual rides peak on Saturday at about 24% and Sunday at 19%, with 10-14% on weekdays. Member rides are 15-17% per weekday and 9-10% per weekend day.

**03_hourly.png**: Two line charts of rides by start hour. On weekdays members peak at 8am and 5pm; casual riders peak at 5pm with no morning peak. On weekends both groups peak between noon and 3pm, casual riders most sharply.

**04_ride_length.png**: Grouped bar chart of ride-length buckets. Most member rides are under 20 minutes. Casual rides are spread out, with 41% over 30 minutes. Median ride: casual 25.8 min, member 9.8 min.

**05_top_stations.png**: Horizontal bar chart of the ten start stations with the most casual rides, led by Streeter Dr & Grand Ave with 53,071 rides. At the top two stations about 78-79% of all rides are casual.
